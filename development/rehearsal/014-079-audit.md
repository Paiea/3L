## Main sequence-level weakness

From Record 014 onward, many chapters repeat a case-study grammar:

1. witty thesis/opening reversal
2. work or institutional problem
3. dialogue-driven diagnosis
4. procedural correction
5. reflective lesson at the end

Individual records are often strong. In sequence, repeated chapter grammar can make the book feel thinner or more procedural than Records 001–010 even when the prose itself remains specific.

Rehearsal should therefore restore scene dominance and formal variation, not merely add words.

## Batch rule

Start formal rehearsal at 014 as requested, but allow a record to pass unchanged when it is already doing its job. Do not confuse `needs_rehearsal` metadata with a mandate to rewrite every sentence.

For each five-record batch:

- read immediate neighbors
- identify the record's unique job
- rehearse only where scene, life, or listen resolution is missing
- keep strong prose intact
- seam-check chapter grammar across the batch
- do not promote canon until user approval
