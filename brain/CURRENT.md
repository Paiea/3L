## First-Life state after Record 050

Greg is about thirty-three. Roughly two years have passed since the second tournament. Time has been spent on contracts, travel, recovery, ordinary support work, study, conditioning, repeat-team relationships, and failed practice rather than only visible breakthroughs.

### Record 045 · THE POCKET
Greg learns why conventional support doctrine favors self-aura, stable protected positioning, predictable target priority, and reserve. He tries to solve a moving support problem by running late and discovers that seeing the useful place is not enough if his body and casting precision cannot reach it cleanly. He begins asking how maintained Barriers are actually defeated.

### Record 046 · THE BREAKER
Late in the north-road contract, Greg watches ward-breaker Mara Quill dismantle an old maintained ward with Draw rather than brute force. He learns that Draw attacks sustaining flow and moves mana rather than simply destroying it. Mara explicitly warns that using aggressive Draw on living aura is not support. The three-month party then actually ends, leaving ordinary absence behind.

### Record 047 · THE DRAIN
Over roughly the next year Greg studies breaker Draw at Stone Yard while continuing ordinary work. He is bad at it for months. His personal aura repeatedly interferes with foreign flow, and lowering aura makes Draw cleaner while making Greg more physically vulnerable. He becomes competent at breaker Draw, not exceptional, and remains warned away from using it as a shortcut for living transfer.

### Record 048 · THE OPENING
On a later south-quarry contract with Orla and Calder, Greg uses a tiny emergency Draw on Orla's overloaded aura while she is holding a shifting slab. The intervention partly works and the mana is immediately reused in one of Greg's Barriers, but Greg violates Orla's consent and cannot measure exactly what he removed. Stone engineer Edda Pell is explicit: Greg saw a real pressure problem but had not earned the intervention. Greg then spends months studying legitimate channel relief and transfer methods instead of repeating the trick casually.

### Record 049 · THE CHASE
Greg asks Arcrutus about switching and mobility. Arcrutus reframes the problem: Greg is already late before he starts moving. Controlled ridge-hound drills force Greg to run, think, maintain fine Barriers, manage aura, and anticipate where support will be needed. Arcrutus leaves after providing the hostile test. Greg spends seasons turning it into road running, hill work, loaded movement, and casting-under-fatigue practice. His visible gain is small but meaningful: three early steps replace one desperate sprint.

### Record 050 · THE BORROWED PART
The old tournament four reunite for a six-week crawler-suppression contract. Greg tests a narrow version of redistribution: with explicit consent, Draw a small amount from one teammate and immediately spend that mana on Greg's own support elsewhere. It is lossy and control-expensive. Drawing from Calder at the correct recovery moment works. Drawing from Veda because she appears idle ruins a prepared burst and forces Calder to cover the mistake. The party creates simple consent/availability signals. By the end Greg has only six live Draw uses, all small. He has found a mechanism, not mastered a network.

## Approved First-Life climb pressure

Durable development direction:

Barrier specialization -> study what defeats Barrier -> Draw as ward-drain/counter-technique -> controlled relief of allied magical pressure -> redistribution/cycling -> linking -> multi-person capability architecture.

The approved design lives in `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-15-first-life-open-circuit-support-design.md` and is mirrored in `brain/OPEN_CIRCUIT.md` on the working branch.

Important constraints:
- conventional support doctrine is rational and often safer;
- competent experts should tell Greg he is wrong, and sometimes they should be completely right about the implementation in front of them;
- Greg's advantage is fast feedback and iteration, not infallibility;
- personal aura/self-reinforcement is normal support doctrine and materially conflicts with tactical Draw-routing;
- Greg's eventual open-circuit style sacrifices passive self-protection for outward flow rather than granting a free efficiency upgrade;
- mobility and extreme stamina matter because proximity improves sensing, transfer, reacquisition, and precision;
- Greg's C-class warrior history gives him a movement chassis, not secret elite combat ability;
- mana cycling never creates mana; it reallocates existing reserve and otherwise-idle regeneration opportunity;
- stronger and more complementary parties give mature Greg more useful margin to allocate, while also increasing control complexity;
- S-class people eventually value Greg because he does not require them to fight like conventional support clients and can release capacity trapped behind their actual bottlenecks;
- Arcrutus may puncture Greg's framing or provide hostile practical tests, but he does not become Greg's conventional mentor;
- the First-Life climb is relational and outward. Life-Two revenge later turns the same intelligence toward increasingly closed/self-contained loops, with the ruined leg compounding a divergence Greg already chose.

## Pressure for Record 051

Do not jump immediately to a stable mana network or a rank promotion.

Greg now has a primitive, lossy redistribution mechanism and a small repeat-team vocabulary for consent/availability. The next useful pressure is to let months and contracts prove what survives outside Calder/Orla/Veda. Useful possibilities include:
- Greg discovering that day-one work with strangers is much worse than repeat-team work;
- formal support or breaker professionals criticizing his narrow specialization and poor deployability;
- Greg beginning to measure teammate demand/recovery rhythms rather than assuming visible reserve is surplus;
- first attempts to reduce switching/transfer loss without pretending foreign mana has become interchangeable;
- ordinary relationships and contract endings continuing to matter while Greg's reputation grows slowly.

Do not make Greg B/A/S merely because the mechanism has appeared. Rank should follow repeated operational evidence.
