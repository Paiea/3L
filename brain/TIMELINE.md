# TIMELINE

> Calendar and life-state ledger. Canonical prose wins every conflict.

## Present frame

- Life Two Greg is about 59.
- He reaches the ancient dragon Ithar while the western Line is failing.
- The examination opens the record of his two lives.

## Life Two

### Age 19

**Record 004 · THE RETURNED**
- Greg wakes nineteen again with First-Life memory.
- His dead family is alive.
- His first instinct is reconstruction: rebuild smarter, faster, cleaner.

**Record 005 · THE ASPIRANT**
- Greg skips the western ridge contract he remembers taking in First Life.
- He accepts lower-paid East Four maintenance because home exists again.

### Age 19 into later twenties

**Record 006 · THE HAND**
- A four-week East Four job becomes eleven years of life.
- Work, family routine, and Nessa become ordinary rather than provisional.
- Greg's optimization drive erodes under actual living.

**Record 007 · THE ADVISER**
- Remembered knowledge prevents an East Four bypass disaster.
- Halden survives.
- Greg learns that remembering an outcome is not the same as understanding its mechanism.
- History begins diverging materially.

**Record 008 · THE RESCUER**
- Greg remembers Nessa dying in a First-Life East Four pressure disaster.
- He tries to prevent the remembered cause and initially makes the event worse.
- Buried old Line infrastructure is uncovered.
- Nessa survives.
- Greg acquires a lasting left-shoulder click before rain.

### Around age 30

**Record 009 · THE STAYER**
- Greg declines a lucrative western climb contract.
- His reason is simple: he already lived that life.
- Ordinary Life Two becomes the chosen path.

**Record 010 · THE STRANGER**
- Bren Calder appears.
- Bren was Greg's chosen brother in First Life but does not know him here.
- Halden's survival prevented the North Vey rupture that shaped Bren's First-Life path.
- Bren is A-class by a different route.
- Greg refuses to manufacture the friendship.

## Time-use rule

3L spans decades. Burn real calendar time. Every canonical record should make elapsed time legible enough that Greg's body, work, money, family, relationships, and world can age instead of remaining in chapter-time suspension.
