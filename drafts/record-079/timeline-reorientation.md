This is a staging note for the active `rehearsal.md` candidate. It does not replace manuscript authority.

Structural rule: Greg owns the association. Ithar owns the coordinates.

The chapter may connect ideas across lives and years instead of proceeding as a strict chronology. Ithar should periodically establish the state of the story even when a careful reader could infer it. Do not turn the anchors into title cards.

## Return to present cave

After any substantial remembered block, do not return with abstract narration alone. Restore the room physically:

- Ithar shifts a claw or tail.
- stone grates
- Greg's present damaged body complains when he changes position
- cave light catches Ithar's glass-dark surface
- a pause has lasted longer in the room than it felt inside Greg's memory

Then let Ithar summarize the coordinate he believes he has reconstructed. This can reveal misunderstandings Greg needs to correct.

## Rhythm

Do not anchor every memory. Anchor material state changes.

Strong triggers:
- First Life ↔ Second Life
- remembered scene ↔ present Ithar cave
- before children ↔ after children
- before Vey ↔ after Vey
- before/after S-class recognition
- before/after family catastrophe
- a person appears in a life where they do not share the relationship Greg remembers

One or two precise coordinates are enough. Repetition is useful when the timeline is genuinely complex, not as a verbal tic.
